Practical field reference

Agriculture in Ethiopia

A practical starting point for planning crops, caring for soil and water, and keeping farm records.

Growing seasons and suitable crops vary by region, altitude, rainfall, and local conditions. Use this guide as general information and confirm timing with local agricultural extension workers.

Field practices

Plan around the season

Check local rainfall forecasts and the crop calendar before choosing planting dates. Prepare seed, labor, and equipment early, and keep a backup plan for delayed or uneven rain.

Protect soil and choose seed well

Use locally adapted, quality seed. Where practical, rotate crops, retain ground cover, add compost or other suitable organic matter, and follow soil-test or extension advice for fertilizer.

Use water carefully

Record irrigation dates and amounts. Reduce runoff and erosion with methods suited to the field, and prioritize water for sensitive crop stages when supplies are limited.

Scout for pests and disease

Inspect fields regularly and note where symptoms appear. Start with prevention and locally recommended integrated pest management; use registered products only as directed, with protective equipment.

A simple season checklist

  1. 1

    Before planting

    Review the plan, check seed and inputs, and prepare fields and tools.

  2. 2

    Planting

    Use locally advised timing, spacing, and planting depth.

  3. 3

    During growth

    Check moisture, weeds, crop health, and pest signs; record work and costs.

  4. 4

    Harvest and storage

    Harvest at suitable maturity, dry and store produce safely, and record quantity and sales.

Keep records that help decisions

Track crop, field, date, input type and quantity, harvest quantity, sales, and expenses. Consistent records help compare crops and plan the next season.
